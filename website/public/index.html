Built for service

One Main phone. A whole floor of stations.

Order Flow is not a cloud till. The licensed Main device is the shop server. Tablets and phones connect on the same Wi‑Fi. If the internet drops, the shop still sells — and on custom plans, an encrypted cloud relay keeps every station in sync even when the shop Wi‑Fi itself dies, with no shop data ever stored in the cloud.

Kitchen fire

Send to kitchen prints the slip from Main and alerts the pass with sound, vibration and a banner. Tickets age live — amber at 10 minutes, red at 20.

Roles that fit

Manager, order taker, kitchen, cashier, driver, stock, front desk, specialist — cover from Main when you need to.

Wi‑Fi down? Service goes on

Custom plans get cloud networking: Main opens an encrypted room, each station joins on its own mobile data. Orders queue offline and merge automatically — the shop keeps selling. (Your data stays on Main; the cloud is only a corridor — not storage.)

Your face on the QR page

Give guests an ordering page with your shop's name, tagline, hours, WhatsApp button and colour — a beautiful animated menu that works on every phone, Urdu included. A custom-plan touch.

Drivers

Visual Table Map & Seating

Questions shops ask

Clear answers.

Does my sales data go to the cloud?

No. Tickets, menu and stock live on Main. The website only checks the license and serves this app file.

What if the internet drops?

Selling continues on shop Wi‑Fi. Stations can queue work and flush when Main is reachable. License has an offline grace window.

How do I install the app?

Web (iPhone & Android): open jathol.org/app in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) → Add to Home Screen → open from the new icon. Works offline. Android APK: tap Google Play above → “Coming soon” → Download APK directly → allow install once. Windows: Download Windows ZIP below.

Can I try before I buy?

Yes. Fill the 3-day trial form — a draft opens on this page. Send it from Gmail or your mail app and Jathol replies within 24 hours with your trial key.

How do I buy a key?

Email contact@jathol.org with your name, business and phone — Jathol issues the 16-character key, normally within 24 hours.

What does it cost?

Starter is RM 79/month with 12 features and optional QR & cloud add-ons; Customize is bespoke with all 15 features. See the Plans section — the license decides which features your key unlocks.

What hardware does it work with?

Web: prints via Apple’s system print dialog (AirPrint) on iPhone — tap Print with Apple and pick your AirPrint/Bluetooth printer (pair it in iOS Settings → Bluetooth first). Android web can also pair raw Bluetooth. If iPhone has no printer, the web app can relay-print to an Android station that has a printer. Any Android phone/tablet, LAN or Bluetooth ESC/POS printers, barcode guns, cash drawer on RJ11.

Where do I learn every feature?

The full step-by-step guide is on the User guide page — updated with every app version.
