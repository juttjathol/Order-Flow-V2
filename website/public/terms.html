2 · The license, plainly

  • A license key activates one Main device per shop. Stations (kitchen, cashier, takers, drivers) on your premises join that Main under the same key.
  • Plans are monthly and manual: RM 79 (Starter) or RM 149 (Growth) — larger shops quote with us directly. There is no automatic renewal and no card on file. If a paid period ends without renewal, Main locks itself until a new key is issued — sales records stay intact on the device.

  • Feature tiers are controlled by the key, not the APK — a Growth key unlocks the Growth feature set on your Main.
