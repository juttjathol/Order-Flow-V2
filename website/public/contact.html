What to include

  • Your licence key (16 characters) or the phone number that paid — it lets us find you instantly.
  • Device ID from the app's Licence screen — needed for any binding reset.
  • Android version and printer model for printing problems.
  • A screenshot of any error, if you can — it shortens the back-and-forth.
  • Never send card or banking passwords. We only ever need your key, your phone number and the amount paid.

Business details

Order Flow is built, licensed and supported by Jathol, trading from Malaysia. We are a software vendor, not a walk-in counter, so all support runs by email — that keeps a written record for both sides. Our company registration number and registered address are printed on every licence receipt and invoice, and are provided by email on request before you buy, as required by the Consumer Protection (Electronic Trade Transactions) Regulations 2012. Prices are quoted in Malaysian Ringgit; billing and refunds follow the refund policy.

Prefer to read before you write? The user guide covers installation, stations, printing and troubleshooting, the plans page lists what each licence switches on, and privacy questions are answered in the privacy policy.
