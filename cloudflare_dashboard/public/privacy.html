Order Flow privacy policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Order Flow (Jathol) is an offline-first point-of-sale app. This page is the public privacy policy for Google Play and for users of the app.

Who we are

The app is published by Jathol. Support: WhatsApp @Jathol_Jutt.

What stays on the shop device

Menu items and photos, prices, stock, tickets, customers, staff, printers, and settings are stored on the Main phone. Stations talk to Main over the shop Wi‑Fi. We do not host your live sales in the cloud.

What is sent online

  • License check: license key and a random device ID are sent to https://order-flow-v2.pages.dev so the Main device can activate and stay valid.
  • Optional: if you tap WhatsApp support, WhatsApp receives the message you send.
  • We do not use advertising IDs, analytics SDKs, or sell shop data.

Permissions

  • Camera — barcode scan and join QR (optional).
  • Bluetooth — receipt / kitchen printers (optional).
  • Location / nearby Wi‑Fi — Android may require this to read the shop Wi‑Fi IP so stations can join Main. We do not map your location.
  • Notifications / foreground service — keeps the shop server running on Main.

Data deletion

Uninstall the app to remove local shop data from that phone. To reset a license binding, contact WhatsApp @Jathol_Jutt with your device ID (More → License).

Children

Order Flow is a business POS tool. It is not directed at children under 13.
